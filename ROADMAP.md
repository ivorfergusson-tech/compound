# Roadmap: later, not now

Ideas agreed on 29 Oct 2026 but deliberately **not** being built yet. The current plan is `PLATFORM.md`. Nothing here starts without asking Ivor, and anything that costs money needs an adult or a company behind it.

| Area | Later | Why it waits |
|---|---|---|
| Data protection | Cyber Essentials certification; listing on the DfE and LGfL buying lists | Paid, and needs a legal entity |
| School systems | MIS sync through Wonde; school single sign-on | Wonde is paid; SSO needs school IT on board |
| Proof it works | A case study page, written with the pilot teacher's approval | Needs real pilot results first (P12) |
| Subjects | GCSE Economics and Business (much shared content), then Edexcel and OCR A-levels | AQA A-levels first |
| Video | Optional short explainer videos embedded from YouTube | Step-through diagrams come first |
| Human help | Peer help; paid tutoring | Safeguarding and moderation load |
| Parents | Weekly progress emails to parents | Email volume and consent |
| Phone app | App Store and Play Store wrappers around the PWA | Developer fees; PWA first |
| Reliability | Supabase Pro and a paid school plan | Only when the cost sheet in SETUP.md says it's needed |
