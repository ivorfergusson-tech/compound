Privacy

Compound is a free learning site made by Ivor Fergusson, with a personal finance course, A-level courses and tools for school classes. This page explains, in plain English, what it stores about you and what you can do about it. Last updated 31 October 2026.

Without an account

You can use the whole course without signing up. Your progress, quiz answers and notes are saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

If you make an account (with an email and password, Google or Microsoft), we store:

  • your email address, only so you can sign in;
  • the nickname you choose and when you last changed it (nicknames can change once every 7 days);
  • which modules you've read, your quiz and checkpoint attempts and scores, the questions you got wrong (so they come back in recall checks), your "In my own words" notes and guesses, the days you were active (for your streak), your display settings (font, text size, spacing, contrast) and any exam dates you add to the dashboard countdown, so they follow you to any device.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use only the email address to identify your account. We don't use or show your name or picture.

Your data isn't shared unless you choose to join a class. Other users can't see it: each account can only read its own rows; this is enforced by the database, not just the website. The public leaderboard shows only your nickname, level and XP. Don't use your real name as a nickname.

Classes

A teacher can invite you to a class, and you choose whether to join. Before you join, we tell you what your teacher will see and ask you to agree. If you join:

  • your teacher sees the real name you give for that class, your nickname, your scores, your streak and when you were last active, for that class's course only;
  • for work they set, your teacher also sees your answers, your marks, how many tries you took, when you started and finished, and roughly how long you spent, on the whole piece of work and on each question. Time is measured in your browser and stops when the page is hidden or left idle for 2 minutes;
  • your teacher sees any past paper marks you log after joining, and an estimate of the grade you're working at, worked out from them;
  • your teacher can download your name and marks as a spreadsheet;
  • your teacher sees only your work from the day you joined, and nothing from before;
  • other students see your nickname and class XP, and never your real name;
  • you can leave at any time. Your teacher keeps your past marks, but sees nothing new.

If you're a teacher, we store your name, your school if you give it, whether your account is verified, and the classes you make. Students who join your classes see your name and school.

Photos of your work

If your teacher sets written work, you can hand it in as photos. They're kept in private storage that only you and your teacher can open. They're deleted automatically 90 days after your work was handed in or last marked, and overnight if you remove them or delete your account. Please don't write your name on the pages.

AI marking

AI marking and reading handwriting are switched off at the moment, so nothing of yours is sent to an AI company. If we turn them on, they'll use Anthropic's Claude, and we'll update this page first to say exactly what's sent. The plan is to send the question, the mark scheme and your answer or photos, without your name. Any AI mark would be labelled as an estimate, and your teacher's mark would be the one that counts.

Past papers and reports

If you log past paper marks, we store the paper, the date, your marks and the topics you chose. If you report a problem with a question, we store the question, the reason and any note you add. Reviewers see the report but not who sent it, so please don't put personal details in a note.

Who holds it

Data is stored by Supabase, our database provider, on servers in London (UK). Your password is stored by Supabase only in scrambled (hashed) form; we never see it. We don't sell or share your data, and there are no ads or tracking scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Read aloud uses your browser's built-in voices: some browsers (Chrome, for example) use their maker's online voices, which sends the text being read to them.

Why we can hold it

Under UK GDPR, we process your data because you asked us to provide your account (to keep your progress and show you on the leaderboard). We keep it until you delete your account.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: Account → Export my data downloads everything as a file.
  • Delete it: Account → Delete my account removes your login and your records, straight away and for good. Photos go the same night. Three things stay, as none of them says who you are: reports you sent, with the link to you removed; a running count of AI use, kept for the spending record, which names nobody; and any marks a teacher downloaded before you left, which are the school's to look after.
  • Correct it: change your notes at any time, and your nickname once every 7 days.
  • Leave a class: Your classes → Leave class, at any time.

You can also ask the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you're unhappy with how your data is handled.

Not financial advice

Compound is education only. It doesn't know your situation and doesn't recommend any product. Trading is taught with pretend money only.
