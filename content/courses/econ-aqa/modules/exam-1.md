:::callout idea
You don't get marks for what you know. You get marks for showing four skills, in the right amounts, in the time you have.
:::

## The three papers

A-level Economics has three exams. Each lasts **2 hours**, has **80 marks** and counts for a **third** of your grade.

| Paper | Covers | Section A | Section B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1: Markets and market failure | Microeconomics | Data response: choose 1 of 2 contexts (40 marks) | Essay: choose 1 of 3 (40 marks) |
| Paper 2: National and international economy | Macroeconomics | Data response: choose 1 of 2 contexts (40 marks) | Essay: choose 1 of 3 (40 marks) |
| Paper 3: Economic principles and issues | Everything | 30 multiple choice questions (30 marks) | A case study investigation (50 marks) |

Section A on Papers 1 and 2 has been split into questions worth **2, 4, 9 and 25** marks. Each Section B essay has parts worth **15 and 25** marks. Paper 3's investigation has been split into **10, 15 and 25** marks. AQA can change a paper's layout, so check a recent paper.

## Timing

You have 120 minutes for 80 marks, which is **a mark every 90 seconds**.

| Marks | About how long |
|---|---|
| 2 | 3 minutes |
| 4 | 6 minutes |
| 9 | 13 minutes |
| 15 | 22 minutes |
| 25 | 37 minutes |

That leaves very little spare, and you also need to read the data and choose your questions. Most lost marks come from running out of time on the last 25-marker, which is worth far more than a few extra lines elsewhere.

## The four skills

AQA marks four [[assessment objectives]]:

- **AO1 Knowledge:** know the terms, concepts and theories.
- **AO2 Application:** use them on this context, with its data.
- **AO3 Analysis:** explain why, in linked steps, from cause to effect.
- **AO4 Evaluation:** weigh the arguments and reach a supported judgement.

Across the whole A-level, these are close to evenly weighted, at between 20% and 29% each. So **AO1 alone won't get you far**. A long list of correct facts with no analysis or judgement stays in the lower levels.

## Command words

A [[command word]] tells you which skills to show.

| Word | What it wants |
|---|---|
| Calculate | A number, with working and the right unit |
| Explain | Reasons, in linked steps |
| Using a diagram | A correct, labelled diagram that you use in your writing |
| Analyse | Break it down, and trace the causes and effects |
| Assess, evaluate, discuss, to what extent | Both sides, and a supported judgement |

Underline the command word and the key term before you write. Answers that are true but off the question score poorly.
