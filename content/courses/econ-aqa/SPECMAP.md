# AQA A-level Economics (7136): spec map

**Status: approved by Ivor Fergusson, 30 Oct 2026.** Built from the official specification, version 1.3 (2022-06-29), downloaded and checked 2026-10-30: <https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/economics/specifications/AQA-7135-7136-SP-2015.PDF>. Section 4 (the full A-level) only; spec point numbers and headings are AQA's.

79 spec points → **88 lessons in 16 modules**, with 4 checkpoints. At 6 bank questions per spec point that's about 474 questions, plus 25-markers per section.

## What to check

1. **Merges** (two tiny points taught as one lesson): 4.1.1.2 + 4.1.1.3; 4.1.4.1 + 4.1.4.2.
2. **Splits** (too big for one 5–10 minute lesson): 4.1.5.5 into 2; 4.2.3.1 into 2; 4.2.3.2 into 2; 4.2.3.3 into 2; 4.2.4.3 into 2; 4.2.5.1 into 3; 4.2.6.2 into 2; 4.2.6.3 into 2; 4.2.6.5 into 2; 4.1.8.9 into 2. The part titles are mine; the spec has one heading.
3. **Module splits:** 4.1.5 (12 lessons) and 4.1.8 (11) each become two modules. The module titles for the halves are mine.
4. **AS column:** the AS point with the same heading (section 3 of the same spec). Title match only, so a guide to what's usually taught in Year 12, not a rule. Your school's order wins: tell me what it teaches when.
5. **Checkpoints:** cp1 after 4.1.1–4.1.4; cp2 after 4.1.5a–4.1.8b; cp3 after 4.2.1–4.2.3; cp4 after 4.2.4–4.2.6.
6. **Order:** spec order (micro 4.1, then macro 4.2). Schools often mix the two each year; a teacher will be able to set their own order in P4.
