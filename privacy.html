Privacy

Compound is a free finance course made by Ivor Fergusson. This page explains, in plain English, what it stores about you and what you can do about it. Last updated 27 October 2026.

Without an account

You can use the whole course without signing up. Your progress, quiz answers and notes are saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

If you make an account (with an email link or Google), we store:

  • your email address, only so you can sign in;
  • the nickname you choose;
  • which modules you've read, your quiz and checkpoint attempts and scores, the questions you got wrong (so they come back in recall checks), your "In my own words" notes and guesses, and the days you were active (for your streak).

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use only the email address to identify your account. We don't use or show your name or picture.

Your data is never shared. Other users can't see it: each account can only read its own rows; this is enforced by the database, not just the website. The public leaderboard shows only your nickname, level and XP. Don't use your real name as a nickname.

Who holds it

Data is stored by Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the EU (London region). We don't sell or share your data, and there are no ads or tracking scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Why we can hold it

Under UK GDPR, we process your data because you asked us to provide your account (to keep your progress and show you on the leaderboard). We keep it until you delete your account.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: Account → Export my data downloads everything as a file.
  • Delete it: Account → Delete my account removes your login and every record, straight away and for good.
  • Correct it: change your nickname or notes at any time.

You can also ask the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you're unhappy with how your data is handled.

Not financial advice

Compound is education only. It doesn't know your situation and doesn't recommend any product. Trading is taught with pretend money only.
